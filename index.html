Computer Science + Product Systems

Sri Harshitha Jampani

I'm a Computer Science major with an unhealthy curiosity for understanding why products are built the way they are. I often find myself wondering what happened in the meeting room that led to that feature being prioritized, or that flow being redesigned while using an app.

On a side note, I genuinely enjoy being in the room where those conversations happen - where people debate what should be built, what shouldn't, and why that feature would enhance user experience. Writing long PRDs and user stories is just a price I pay for a seat at that table.

My curiosity often sends me down rabbit holes at 3 am. One minute I'm looking up how a feature works, and the next I'm reading engineering blogs and architecture deep dives. I especially love case studies that read like battle scars. "My Role in Redesigning a Checkout Flow Under Extreme Legacy Technical Constraints" is exactly my kind of bedtime story. Someday, I hope to have enough scars of my own to write one.

Until then, my weekends are usually spent binge-watching HLD and LLD playlists with the same enthusiasm I have for a Netflix series. I occasionally write breakdowns of products and apps that catch my attention, and I read far too many books. Yet, Ayn Rand's The Fountainhead remains a favorite. When I'm not overanalyzing products or systems, you'll probably find me in the gym trying to maintain a healthy balance between technical debt and mental health.

My Story

About

I'm still early in my career, so what matters most to me is meaningful work and being around people who enjoy solving hard problems.

As a Computer Science student, I bring both a technical and product lens. I enjoy understanding why products are built the way they are, debating trade-offs, and thinking through what creates value for users. My approach is research-first - I prefer understanding user behavior, goals, and pain points before jumping into solutions.

One edge I bring is my hands-on experience building AI products. Having worked with LLMs, agentic systems, RAG pipelines, and production AI workflows, I can bridge the gap between what's technically possible and what's genuinely useful for users. I enjoy working at the intersection of technology, business, and user experience.

I thrive in teams where decisions are discussed, challenged, and backed by reasoning rather than simply handed down. For me, the most exciting part of product building is identifying the right problem to solve and figuring out the best way to solve it.
